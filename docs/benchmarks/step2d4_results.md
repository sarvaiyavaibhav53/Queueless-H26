## 1. Methodology

This benchmark evaluates queue intervention policies under strict methodological controls:
- **Two-Phase Architecture**: Complete separation between the **Decision Phase** (action selection) and the **Evaluation Phase** (action scoring).
- **Random Seed Separation**: Decision seeds and Evaluation seeds are drawn from disjoint, non-overlapping deterministic arithmetic progressions.
- **Common Random Numbers (CRN)**: Within the Evaluation Phase, Baseline, Naive-30, Naive-15, and the chosen PRAVAAH action share the identical evaluation seed per replication for variance-reduced paired statistical tests.
- **Sample Size**: N = 100 decision replications; N = 100 evaluation replications per scenario.
- **Primary Objective**: Minimization of cumulative waiting person-minutes, including backlog accrued by citizens still queued at the simulation horizon.
- **P90 Constraints**: Feasibility constraints enforced per service; infeasible interventions are rejected.

## 2. Stability Thresholds

- **Threshold Origin**: **Newly introduced synthetic project assumptions** (no pre-existing shared stability classifier existed in the codebase prior to Step 2D.4).
- **Centralized Implementation**: Implemented in [`shared/stability.py`](file:///shared/stability.py) and exported via `shared` package.
- **Bands Defined**:
  - **STABLE**: $\rho < 0.85$ (0.85)
  - **WATCH**: $0.85 \le \rho < 1.00$ (0.85 to 1.00)
  - **UNSTABLE**: $\rho \ge 1.00$ (1.00+)
- **Operational Meaning**: These thresholds are synthetic simulation modeling conventions. They are **not** empirically validated statutory standards.

## 3. Naive Policy

Naive operates as a current-state heuristic with zero future simulation or look-ahead:
1. **Trigger / Target Rule**:
   - Identifies the service with the longest current waiting queue.
   - If the target service is **STABLE** ($\rho < 0.85$) or all queues are empty, returns **no action**.
2. **Donor Selection Rule**:
   - Active counter eligible to serve target and not already assigned to target.
   - **Source Protection Constraint**: The donor's source service must retain at least 1 active assigned counter after reassignment.
   - Ranked deterministically: (1) lowest current source service $\rho$, (2) idle counter preferred on $\rho$ tie, (3) lowest `counter_id` tie-break.
3. **Action Parameters**:
   - Primary (**Naive-30**): 30-minute duration, 3-minute switching delay.
   - Sensitivity (**Naive-15**): 15-minute duration, 3-minute switching delay.
   - Permanent reassignment is forbidden.

## 4. PRAVAAH Decision Rule

During the Decision Phase (N=100 decision replications in Decision World):
1. Evaluates no action and legal intervention candidates (15m, 30m; 3m switching delay).
2. Filters candidates violating service-level P90 SLA constraints.
3. Selects the feasible candidate minimizing mean waiting person-minutes.
4. **Decision Margin Gate** (Intervention recommended ONLY if BOTH pass):
   - **Condition A (10% Improvement)**: $\text{improvement} = \frac{\text{baseline\_mean} - \text{candidate\_mean}}{\text{baseline\_mean}} \ge 0.10$
   - **Condition B (70% Paired Win Rate)**: Candidate has lower waiting time than baseline in $\ge 70$ out of 100 decision replications.
5. If either condition fails, or if no candidate is feasible, returns **"No intervention required"**.

## 5. Information Boundary

- **Decision World**: Uses snapshot state, observed arrival history, base configured arrival profile, and future events explicitly flagged with `announced=True`.
- **Hidden from Decision World**: Unannounced future arrival surges (`announced=False`) and unannounced counter outages (`announced=False`). Unannounced burst multipliers do NOT alter the decision arrival rate.
- **Scoring World**: Simulates the complete scenario execution, including both announced and unannounced events.

## 6. Previous Look-Ahead Visibility Audit

> [!WARNING]
> **Forensic Finding — Previous Codebase Information Leakage**  
> In previous versions of Step 2D.4, `ScenarioConfig.build_evaluator_config()` passed unconstrained `availability_events` and raw `arrival_bursts` directly into `InterventionEvaluator`.  
> As a consequence, in Scenario E (counter failure at $t=10$) and Scenario D (BC arrival burst at $t=30$), PRAVAAH's decision simulator was able to "see" unannounced future failures and bursts before they occurred.  
> **Previous benchmark improvements in Scenarios D and E that relied on this leakage must NOT be treated as valid evidence of predictive look-ahead.**  
> In this corrected benchmark, unannounced events are strictly filtered from the Decision World.

## 7. Horizon-End Waiting Semantics

- **Accrued Wait Formulation**: For every citizen still waiting in queue at simulation horizon $T$, accrued waiting time is computed as $\max(0.0, T - t_{\text{arrival}})$.
- **Integrity Preservation**: The primary objective `total_waiting_person_minutes` = $\text{served\_wait} + \text{unserved\_accrued\_wait}$.
- **Verification**: Forensic inspection confirmed `DiscreteEventEngine` already includes accrued unserved wait in `total_waiting_person_minutes`. No policy can artificially minimize waiting time by abandoning citizens at the horizon.
